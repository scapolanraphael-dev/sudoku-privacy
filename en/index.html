Privacy Policy

Sudoku app — com.quarktop.games.sudoku

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This page explains what data the Sudoku app processes, why, and how you stay in control of it. It is written to be read, not to hide behind jargon.

In short: your games, your streak and your statistics never leave your phone. The only collection that leaves the device comes from advertising, and in Europe it does not start until you have agreed — an agreement you can withdraw at any time from the app's settings.

1.Who is responsible

The app is published by Raphaël Scapolan, an independent sole-trader developer operating under the name Quarktop, acting as data controller under the GDPR. For any question about this policy or your data: rscapdev@gmail.com.

2.What stays on your device

The following is stored locally, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to anyone — not to us, not to a third party — and we have no access to it:

  • your games in progress and completed, your daily streak, your statistics (win rate, best times);
  • your settings: theme, language, contrast, sound, vibration, mistake allowance;
  • the time and days of your daily reminder.

Reminders are local notifications: your phone schedules them itself. No server is involved, and no notification reports anything back to us.

Uninstalling the app erases all of it. The "Reset statistics" button in settings erases the statistics immediately.

3.What advertising collects

The app is free and funded by advertising. Ads are served by Appodeal and its partner ad networks. To select and measure ads, these companies may process:

DataWhat it is used for
Android advertising ID (AAID) A resettable device identifier, used to cap ad repetition and measure performance
IP address Security, and to infer an approximate location (country, region) for relevant ads
Technical device information Model, Android version, language, carrier, screen size — to serve a suitable ad format
Ad interactions Impressions, clicks, videos watched through — billing and measurement
Diagnostics Technical errors from the ad kit, so it keeps working

Never collected: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or GPS location. The app requires no account and requests no location permission — the approximate location mentioned above is inferred from the IP address by our providers, not read from the device. No account identifier and no purchase history is passed to them: the app has neither.

4.Your consent, and how to withdraw it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form appears on first launch, before the ad kit starts. Nothing is collected for advertising purposes until you have answered. The form is built on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2), through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Consent is only meaningful if it can be taken back. You can reopen the form and change your choice at any time:

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences

Declining personalised advertising costs you no feature of the game: non-personalised ads continue to be shown.

Independently of the app, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

5.Legal bases

  • Consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)): reading and writing information on your device for advertising purposes, and personalised advertising.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)): serving non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and the technical operation of the ad kit.

The data described in section 2 is not processed by us at all: it does not leave your device.

6.Who data is shared with

The data in section 3 is passed to Appodeal, acting as an advertising intermediary, and to the ad networks taking part in the auction. The list of partners and their purposes is available inside the consent form itself.

  • Appodeal privacy policy: appodeal.com/privacy-policy
  • Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell any data, and pass none to any third party outside this advertising chain.

7.Transfers outside the European Union

Our advertising providers are established outside the European Union, notably in the United States. Such transfers rely on the mechanisms of Chapter V of the GDPR — the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details are in the providers' policies linked in section 6.

8.Retention

Local data (section 2) is kept until you erase it or uninstall the app. Advertising data (section 3) is kept by the relevant providers under their own policies; your consent itself is requested again at least every 13 months.

9.Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time (section 4). To exercise them, write to rscapdev@gmail.com.

One useful clarification: because the app has no accounts, we hold no identifier that could let us find you. A request concerning advertising data must therefore be addressed directly to the providers in section 6, who are the only ones holding it. We will help you reach them if needed.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

10.Children

The game itself suits any age: it is a sudoku, there is nothing unsuitable in it, and its content rating reflects that. This section is not about content — it is about data.

The app is designed and offered for a general audience. It is neither designed nor marketed for children, and does not knowingly collect data about a child. The reason is advertising: the consent mechanism described in section 4 assumes someone able to consent for themselves. In the European Union, below the national age of digital consent — 15 in France — the authorisation of a holder of parental responsibility is required.

If you are a parent and believe a child has used the app and had data about them processed, write to rscapdev@gmail.com: we will act on it and help you reach the providers listed in section 6.

11.Changes

This policy may change, in particular if the app's features or providers change. The last-updated date appears at the top of this page. Substantial changes will be announced in the app.
